# Roadmap

Product direction, not a release schedule. See [README.md](README.md) for
what ships today. Implementation work is tracked in
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/szebert/video-speed-controller/issues).

## Near term

### Overlay customization

- Show or hide individual overlay controls
- Layout for multi-row controls

## Planned

- Seek bar with current time and duration
- Volume, mute, and volume slider
- Fullscreen and Picture-in-Picture
- Caption toggle and track selection when the video supports it
- Loop and A-B loop
- Optional video information panel

## Exploring

- True reverse decode and reverse audio
- Buffered-range visualization
- More advanced overlay layout and grouping

## Release

Before a public Chrome Web Store listing:

- Store listing and screenshots
- Manual compatibility testing on major video sites
